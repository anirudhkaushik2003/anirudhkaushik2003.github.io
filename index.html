Engineering with research depth

I am a software engineer at Microsoft and a former master's researcher at CVIT, IIIT Hyderabad. My work spans applied machine learning, medical image analysis, and the systems work required to ship reliable software in production.

I care about turning difficult technical ideas into tools people can actually use, whether that means building robust pipelines, designing cleaner interfaces, or writing about the work in public.
